# Approved Visual Mockups

These 21 images are the **visual source of truth** for Questly's look and atmosphere (CLAUDE.md §1).

They are **not** a source of truth for behavior. Their text, numbers, and some mechanics were generated
illustratively and in places conflict with `QUESTLY_PRODUCT_SPEC.md`. Where they disagree, the spec wins
(spec §38.20). Known conflicts include: more than six Skills, Easy/Medium/Hard Quest difficulty, user-chosen
rewards, per-objective XP that exceeds the Quest reward, Combat Achievements that award XP/GP, extra Collection
Log and Diary categories, habit/calendar planning, punitive settings, and RuneScape-branded assets.
